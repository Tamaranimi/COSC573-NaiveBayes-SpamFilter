Important note:
All figures and experimental tables in the paper regenerate from the bundled CSV files. The workflow diagram is a vector TikZ figure authored in the paper source. The bundled CSV files preserve the recorded COSC 573 experiment results; regenerating the PDF reproduces the paper's tables and figures without re-running the classifier.

The two instructor messages are the only labeled examples. All other experiments are controlled diagnostics, not an accuracy benchmark.

Note: unlike the COSC 750 paper, COSC 573 has no Stage 4 sections or instructor-specific arXiv mandate; this package only matches that visual style.
